Each change is applied to the simulation on the next step. "Save" writes assets/aminoacidos.json and assets/orgaos.json (the sources of the values at startup). Hover over the headers to see what each column does.

Organ code

Row = promoter amino acid, column = modifier amino acid (the next one in the protein). Each cell chooses the organ and the variant (0–5) that the pair forms; "—" = no organ (the two amino acids stay as normal ones). Synonymous codons give the same organ. A third codon (if it is not a stop) gives the intensity (×0.06 to ×15). It applies to the births and seeds that follow; bodies already made do not change.

Organs

Each variant has its own properties; the length and the mass multiply those of the promoter amino acid; the costs of the variant multiply those of the type.
